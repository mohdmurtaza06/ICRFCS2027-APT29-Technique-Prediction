NEWLY COMPLETED: PUBLIC-SOURCE CROSS-VENDOR SEQUENCE CHECK
The MITRE ATT&CK Evaluations public JSON files were inspected for McAfee, Microsoft, FireEye, CrowdStrike, and Bitdefender from:
https://github.com/mitre-attack/joystick/tree/master/data/evaluations/apt29

For each file, structured Techniques[].Steps[] records were flattened and sorted by parsed SubStep: numeric major step, branch letter, numeric suborder. Each file yielded 140 unique substep labels and 58 unique technique IDs. The ordered (SubStep, TechniqueId) pairs matched exactly across all five vendor files. This closes the narrower question of whether the sequence order is an artifact of one vendor export. It does NOT make the evaluation five campaigns; the files represent the same MITRE-designed APT29 emulation flow. The paper now states this explicitly and cites the public data repository.

A reproducible helper script is included at scripts/reconstruct_apt29_sequence.py. On a computer with internet access, run:
  python scripts/reconstruct_apt29_sequence.py --download-mitre-five-vendors --data-dir data --outdir artifacts
It writes the ordered sequence, machine-readable comparison results, and text summary. Four unit tests passed locally. Those tests exercise sorting, duplicate rejection, and identity/mismatch handling on small fixtures; they are not a download-and-full-data test of the original model experiments.

CRITICAL 2 — CLUSTERING RANKING RULE
The prose describes selecting a cluster and predicting its most frequent other member, but top-3/top-5 require a complete ranked candidate list. Specify the exact algorithm for ranking candidates, resolving ties, and handling an unseen technique/cluster transition. Reproduce the original rule from implementation or rerun with a predeclared rule. Do not invent a new rule after seeing test results and represent it as the original.

MAJOR 3 — SYNTHETIC GENERATOR COUNTS
The reported generator uses 500 lengths said to be sampled uniformly between 20 and 140, reports mean length 75.5, and claims 37,274 next-technique training instances. Verify actual length draws, endpoint convention, random seed, and window/prefix construction from code and saved outputs. The expectation of a discrete uniform draw over 20..140 is 80, but a realized mean differs randomly; this discrepancy alone does not prove an error. Do not alter counts without checking the implementation.

MAJOR 4 — MODEL REPRODUCIBILITY

MAJOR 5 — GENERALIZATION AND UNCERTAINTY
There is one emulation flow and 42 sequentially dependent prediction positions. Cross-vendor reconstruction checks validate the same source sequence, not campaign-level generalization. Run multiple seeds and report variability. Stronger evidence needs independently sourced ordered sequences and campaign-held-out testing. Keep teacher-forced accuracy separate from free-running forecasts. Aggregate results alone do not support significance claims.
